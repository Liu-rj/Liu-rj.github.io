Renjie is a first-year PhD student in the [Data Systems Group](https://dsg.csail.mit.edu/) at [MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/), advised by [Prof. Samuel Madden](https://db.csail.mit.edu/madden/) and [Prof. Mike Cafarella](https://people.csail.mit.edu/michjc/). His research focuses on agentic systems and their applications.

Before MIT, he received his bachelor's and master's degrees in computer science from [Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/), where he was advised by [Prof. Bo Tang](https://acm.sustech.edu.cn/btang/) and [Prof. Xiao Yan](https://yanxiaosunny.github.io/). He was also fortunate to work with [Prof. Jinyang Li](https://jinyangli.github.io/) at NYU and with [Dr. Minjie Wang](https://jermainewang.github.io/) and [Dr. Zhenkun Cai](https://czkkkkkk.github.io/) at Amazon.
